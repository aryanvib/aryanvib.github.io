  1. EMNLP
    VIBE: Video Instruction-aligned Background Music Generation
    Aryan Vijay Bhosale, Vaibhavi Lokegaonkar, Vishnu Raj, and 5 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2026
    Abs arXiv Website

    A text-and-video-to-music generation model that brings semantic control and instruction adherence to video-to-music. VIBE introduces a depth-wise cross-layer conditioning mechanism to overcome the representational bottleneck of static cross-modal conditioning in diffusion-autoregressive architectures, together with a reward-modeling taxonomy that optimizes both verifiable constraints (tempo, key) and subjective qualities (musicality, multimodal alignment) through a five-stage training curriculum.

    Aryan Vijay Bhosale, Harshit Rajgarhia, Akhil Pothanapalli, and 3 more authors

  3. Preprint
    TORUS: A Test of Rendering-Understanding Self-Coherence for Unified Audio Models
    Aryan Vijay Bhosale, Harshit Rajgarhia, Abhishek Mukherji, and 1 more author
    2026
    Abs arXiv Website

    The first self-coherence test for audio-native unified models, asking whether a model’s generation and understanding heads agree about the same audio rather than evaluating each capability in isolation. TORUS comprises 48 three-stage self-coherence tests with 432 six-option questions spanning speech, sound, and music across five task families.
